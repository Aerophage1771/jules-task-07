# Marketing Site Mockup Spec (No Blog)

This spec defines the copy and content structure for the GermaineTutoring marketing surfaces, excluding the blog.

## Global Chrome
- **Global Nav:** Links to Home, Methodology, Programs, Contact. (Blog omitted).
- **CTA:** "Book a Consult" (dispatches `open-booking-modal`).
- **Footer:** Standard links, copyright, privacy policy, terms of service.

## 1. Home (`/`)
- **Hero Band:** Tagline emphasizing clarity and proven methods.
- **Proof Strip:** 
  - 180 perfect LSAT (2020)
  - 500+ students taught
  - 14+ average point improvement
  - 483-lesson course library
- **Primary CTA:** "Book a Consult"

## 2. Methodology (`/methodology`)
- **Philosophy:** Emphasize the core belief: "There is no LSAT magic, only repeatable processes."
- **Approach:** Break down complex reasoning into simple, atomized steps.

## 3. Programs (`/programs`)
- **Tier 1:** 2-Hour Acceleration - For targeted troubleshooting.
- **Tier 2 (Featured):** 8-Hour Elevation - Comprehensive review and strategy rebuild.
- **Tier 3:** Elite Retainer - Full-cycle admissions and test prep support.

## 4. Pilot Details (`/pilot-details`)
- Specific pilot program details, matching the Programs tier formatting.

## 5. Contact (`/contact`)
- **Header:** "Ready to change how you see the LSAT?"
- **Form/Action:** Single contact strip with one clear charge and one CTA.

## 6. Legal (`/privacy`, `/terms-of-service`)
- Standard legal boilerplate templates.
