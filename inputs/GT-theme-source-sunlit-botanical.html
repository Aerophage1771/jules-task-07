Sunlit Botanical

Overview

Art-directed editorial identity with sculptural type, tactile botanical imagery, hazy denim fields, sunflower gold, and broad negative space.

Color

Denim #344E73 anchors the canvas; slate #425C81 supports secondary fields; gold #C9A149 marks focus and active emphasis; blue-black ink #0A1625 carries analytical reading.

Typography

Sculptural editorial display serif; readable instructional sans; mono metadata. Tight display leading and generous sustained-reading leading.
